Enter the Dungeon

Your party descends the crumbling stair beneath the old keep. Water drips somewhere in the dark, and the lantern throws long shadows across walls carved with runes no one has read in a thousand years.

H2 · Jacquard 24 400 · 42.64px / 2.369rem

The Torchlit Corridor

Every twenty paces a sconce flickers to life as you pass, as if the dungeon itself has noticed you. The rogue counts the flagstones under her breath, searching for the one that does not quite sit flush.

H3 · Alegreya 800 · 31.99px / 1.777rem

A Chest Bound in Iron

In the center of the chamber sits a chest wrapped in iron bands and cold to the touch. The lock is shaped like a grinning skull, and the wizard swears it just winked.

H4 · Alegreya 700 italic · 23.99px / 1.333rem

Roll for Initiative

The skull's grin widens. Shadows peel away from the walls and take the shape of skeletal guards, rusty swords raised. Everyone at the table reaches for a d20 at once.

EMPHASIS · Alegreya 600 italic · 18.00px / 1rem

Never split the party, especially in a room that hums. The emphasis style sits inside body copy, so it keeps the body size and changes only weight, style and color.

BODY · Alegreya 400 · 18.00px / 1rem · line-height 1.6

Body text is the base of the scale (ratio0). Every heading size above is this size multiplied by 1.333 one or more times, which is why the jumps between levels feel even and intentional rather than random.
