The Spellbook

An illuminated wizard's grimoire set entirely in Texturina, a variable textura-inspired serif.

The scale

H1 · Texturina 800 · 43.94px / 2.441rem

The Grimoire of Ash

Bound in cracked leather and smelling faintly of smoke, this book has passed through the hands of eleven wizards. Each one added notes in the margins, and at least two of them were not entirely human.

H2 · Texturina 700 · 35.15px / 1.953rem

Chapter I: Minor Cantrips

Begin with small magic. A cantrip asks for little more than a steady voice, a clear mind and the right flick of the wrist. Mastering the small spells first keeps the large ones from mastering you.

H3 · Texturina 600 · 28.13px / 1.563rem

On the Lighting of Candles

Speak the word for flame while picturing the wick, not the fire. Apprentices who picture the fire tend to light the curtains, the cat and occasionally their own eyebrows.

H4 · Texturina 500 italic · 22.50px / 1.25rem

A Warning to Apprentices

Do not read the final page aloud. The last apprentice who tried is now a very well-spoken frog living in the courtyard fountain, and he would like you to learn from his mistake.

EMPHASIS · Texturina 500 italic · 18.00px / 1rem

Words have weight; spoken spells weigh most of all. The emphasis style sits inside body copy, so it keeps the body size and changes only weight, style and color.

BODY · Texturina 400 · 18.00px / 1rem · line-height 1.6

Body text is the base of the scale (ratio0). Every heading size above is this size multiplied by 1.25 one or more times, which is why the jumps between levels feel even and intentional rather than random.

LINK · Texturina 600 + underline · 18.00px / 1rem

Links stay at body size so they sit naturally in a sentence: Turn to the next chapter.
